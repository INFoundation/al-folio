AI@GOV is my PhD project (2025-2028), examining how AI is implemented and used in the Norwegian central government, and how this affects administrative roles, professional judgment, and public sector governance.

## Working papers

- **Job Satisfaction, Turnover Intentions, and AI Chatbot Usage.**
- **Public Servants' Perceived Performance Boosts from AI.**
- **AI Implementation: A Survey Experiment on Digital Sovereignty and AI Use.**
- **Who Uses AI in Public Administration?**
