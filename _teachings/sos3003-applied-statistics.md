## Course Overview

As a Teaching Assistant for SOS3003 at ISS, I was responsible for teaching applied statistics to around 40 Master's and PhD students, and led 18 seminars covering linear and logistic regression using STATA.

Teaching was coordinated with two professors and other course staff, and the course was revised for the following year based on student feedback.
