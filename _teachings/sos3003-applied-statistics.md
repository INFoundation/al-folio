## Course Overview

I was part of the teaching staff in statistics at the master's and PhD levels at the SU faculty, teaching SOS3003 to around 40 Master's and PhD students and leading 18 seminars covering linear and logistic regression using STATA.

Teaching was coordinated with two professors and other course staff, and the course was revised for the following year based on student feedback.
